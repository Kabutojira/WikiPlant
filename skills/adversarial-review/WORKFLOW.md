# Bounded adversarial review (internal module)

For every consequential conclusion, actively identify its strongest plausible alternative and conditions that would falsify it. Plan targeted searches for replication failure, corrections, limitations, contrary measurements or incompatible project conditions. Do not wait for the user to supply criticism.

Use a `ChallengeRecord` tied to the claim and existing attempt/monitoring operation. Allocate a finite portion of the current operation's remaining query/source allowance; this module grants no extra searches or queue attempts. Record planned, invoked and successful queries separately, inspected source IDs, strongest relevant counterevidence with exact evidence links, actual coverage, applicability limits and how the conclusion changed. Retain zero relevant counterevidence as a search outcome, never as proof of truth.

`not_required`, `not_searched`, `searched`, `partial` and `blocked` are distinct. A planned/reserved query is not a successful search. `searched` requires successful coverage of the bounded plan; failures or unavailable tools remain partial/blocked with reasons. Compare the strength and applicability of opposing evidence. Unsupported criticism receives no mandatory equal weight. Strong evidence against an attractive thesis must influence the resulting claim assessment.

If deeper investigation is necessary, create a candidate through the same topic admission and lineage rules. A peripheral topic records an unresolved terminal limitation and cannot spawn a challenge child, recurring refresh or disguised new root. Other deeper challenges consume the ordinary daily budget when selected.

Weekly maintenance proposes overdue validation for consequential single-origin, stale, disputed or never-challenged claims. The configured validation lane offers one of slots 1–5 to eligible overdue validation/contradiction work. Genuine priority-0 work takes precedence. Persist selected IDs and starvation reasons; never invent urgency or use ordinary slots 6–10. Native monitoring remains separately budgeted.
