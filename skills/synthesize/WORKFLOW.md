# Linked wiki synthesis

Combine newly completed monitoring, queued/manual research, maintenance outcomes, calendar facts, and saved notes with the current relevant wiki. Keep source evidence, research records, evolving wiki claims, and the report as distinct layers.

Update stable pages and relationships/backlinks with source/research references and time scopes. Preserve user-authored text and unrecognized valid fields through managed sections/fresh diffs. Do not upgrade a user note or hypothesis to a verified claim. Keep incompatible supported claims visible; classify non-overlapping dated values as temporal change rather than contradiction. For consequential findings, connect affected projects/assumptions and state applicability gaps and validation needs.

For each material finding, save an applicability assessment bound to the actual project assumption/decision IDs and exact evidence references: `applicable`, `potentially_applicable`, `not_applicable` or `unknown`. Compare measured conditions with the project's actual constraints and explain specific mismatches; generic advice to check constraints is incomplete. A credible result outside the project's operating regime is explicitly not applicable. An interesting connection remains a hypothesis until this comparison establishes its scope. Carry corrections and bounded challenge coverage into synthesis independently of report importance and queue priority.
